services and prices

Every price is fixed and listed up front. We never rush a session, because getting your fit right takes the time it takes, and you finish with a written lookbook to shop from. Travel expenses are paid by the client, in addition to the session price.

    how a session works

    1. Pick a service and a day

      Choose what you need, a day that suits you and where you would like to meet. We confirm by email within one working day.

    2. Tell us about your style

      Fill in a short questionnaire and send a few photos of what you wear now.

    3. Meet in person

      We meet one to one, try pieces on and check how shoulders, sleeves and trouser length sit on you, live. No rush, we take the time it needs.

    4. Get your lookbook

      Soon after, you receive outfits, shopping links and fit notes to keep.

    common questions
    
    
    
      
    Who is Hemline for?
    Hemline is made for men who want to dress better, from a first job to a wedding. Every session is built around your build, your lifestyle and your budget.
    
      
    What do you cover?
    Everyday casuals, office wear, suits and wedding outfits, plus shoes and accessories like belts and watches.
    
      
    Why meet in person?
    Seeing how a shirt sits on your shoulders or how trousers break over your shoes is the clearest way to know what suits you. In person we can try pieces on, adjust and decide on the spot.
    
      
    How long does a session take?
    As long as it takes to get your fit right. We never rush, and we plan the day around you.
    
      
    Will I feel rushed?
    No. We go at your pace and take the time needed to find what fits you properly, so you never feel hurried.
    
      
    Which cities do you cover?
    We work across India. Tell us your city and area when you book, and we confirm the meeting place and the travel cost by email.
    
      
    Who pays for travel?
    Travel expenses are paid by the client, in addition to the session price. We confirm the travel cost with you when we confirm your booking.
    
      
    Where do we meet?
    At a place that suits you, such as your home or a public spot you choose. Tell us where when you book, and we confirm the details by email.
    
      
    Do I need to buy new clothes?
    No. Most sessions start with what you already own. We show you new ways to wear it first, then list only the pieces that fill real gaps.
    
      
    What should I prepare?
    Photos of your favourite outfits, a few things you never wear, the shoes you wear most, and a rough monthly clothing budget. The questionnaire after you book walks you through it.
    
      
    Can I change my booking?
    Of course. Just reply to your confirmation email a day or so before, and we will happily move it for free.
    
      
    Will you tell me what to buy and where?
    Yes. Your lookbook includes shopping links across high-street and mid-range stores, matched to the budget you give us.
